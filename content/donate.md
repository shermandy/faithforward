      ### Support Our Mission: Give Hope, Change Lives


      At Faith Forward Ministries, Inc., we operate entirely on community donations and the tireless hearts of volunteers. We have no paid staff. This means 100% of your financial support goes directly into our community programs, providing immediate relief, professional counseling, and safe havens for families in crisis.


      Because we are a registered 501(c)(3) tax-exempt organization, your financial contributions are completely tax-deductible.


      ### Ways to Give


      #### 1. Secure Online Giving


      Make a one-time investment or set up a recurring monthly gift to sustain our programs year-round. Consistent monthly giving allows us to plan ahead, expand our outreach, and say "yes" to more families in need.

      #### 3. Donate Supplies & Material Goods


      Our hands-on programs depend heavily on physical items. We are always collecting clean, high-quality supplies to distribute directly to local families and individuals experiencing hardships.


      **For Angel Arms (Infant Care):** Diapers (all sizes), baby wipes, infant formula, and new clothing.


      **For Outreach Kits:** Unopened hygiene products (shampoo, soap, toothpaste, deodorant), new socks, blankets, and shelf-stable food items for emergency grocery boxes.
