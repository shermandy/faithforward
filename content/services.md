      At Faith Forward Ministries, Inc., we provide a holistic, compassionate approach to recovery, mental health, and family stabilization. Our services are 100% community-supported, confidential, and designed to meet individuals exactly where they are; without judgment or stigma.


      ### Professional Counseling & Support Groups


      Healing requires addressing the emotional and psychological roots of trauma, stress, and substance use. We offer professional, compassionate guidance for individuals and families navigating life's hardest chapters.


      * Faith-Based Therapy: Confidential, professional mental health and addiction counseling led by a licensed therapist. We integrate clinical expertise with spiritual support to promote deep, lasting healing.

      * Peer Support Groups: Safe spaces where individuals can share their journeys, find accountability, and heal alongside a community that truly understands the realities of recovery.

      * Family Support Navigation: Active addiction affects the whole family. We offer resources, guidance, and counseling specifically tailored to help parents, children, and loved ones heal together.


      ### Angel Arms Infant & Children Recovery Program


      Our most vulnerable community members deserve a safe, nurturing environment to heal and thrive. 


      * Neonatal Abstinence Syndrome (NAS) Support: Specialized, compassionate care and resources for infants born experiencing substance withdrawal.

      * Safe Haven Environment: Temporary transitional support, safe environment housing, and care packages for young children caught in the crossfire of family substance use crises.

      * Crisis Supply Care: Distribution of essential infant and childcare items (diapers, formula, clothing, pack-and-plays) to biological, foster, or kinship families stepping up to care for children in recovery.


      ### Project Stand: Awareness & Education


      We believe that breaking the stigma surrounding substance use disorders requires proactive community education and visible solidarity.


      * Community Awareness Campaigns: Our signature Project Stand events unite thousands of Westmoreland County residents to stand visually and vocally against the stigma of addiction.

      * Educational Workshops: We provide custom presentations for local schools, civic organizations, and churches focusing on prevention, recognizing the signs of substance use, and supporting recovery.

      * Stigma Reduction Training: Equipping community leaders and businesses with the language and resources needed to create supportive environments for individuals in recovery.


      ### Emergency Outreach & Resource Navigation


      When an individual or family is in crisis, getting immediate access to basic necessities is the first step toward stability.


      * Emergency Groceries & Food Distribution: Providing shelf-stable food boxes and emergency grocery supplies to local households experiencing extreme poverty or sudden displacement.

      * Random Gifts of Kindness: A boots-on-the-ground outreach initiative where volunteers distribute physical supplies, hygiene kits, clothing, and hope directly to individuals experiencing homelessness or housing insecurity.

      * Resource Referral Network: Acting as a bridge to other regional resources, helping individuals navigate housing assistance, medical care, and detox/rehabilitation placements.
